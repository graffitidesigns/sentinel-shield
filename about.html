ABOUT SENTINELSHIELD

SentinelShield was developed to make it easier for small businesses to increase and improve their cybersecurity standards. We want to not only make it easier for small businesses to obtain strong security for the data and devices that are part of their business, but also to make it an all in one stop.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to make it so that each and every small business has the opportunity to utilize a variety of cybersecurity services to protect their business in its entirety.

OUR VALUES

01
Simplicity

You tell us what you need, and we'll do the rest. We want to make it easy for small businesses to have a large security team behind them.

02
Transparency

We will always keep you in the know regarding everything surrounding your data, devices, network, and overall security.

03
Reliability

Security should be dependable when businesses need it most. Our services are designed around consistent monitoring, timely alerts, and clear guidance when issues arise.

BUILT FOR SMALL BUSINESSES

Small businesses can face unique challenges when trying to secure their systems, networks, and data. Limited budgets, smaller teams, and unfamiliarity with cybersecurity can make implementing the right protections feel overwhelming.

We specialize in working with small businesses. This is where we come into play. We have all of what you could need in one place.
